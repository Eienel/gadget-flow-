# GadgetFlow — How It Works

A short, plain-English walkthrough of GadgetFlow for everyone who will touch it:
**admins**, **suppliers**, and **buyers**.

---

## What is GadgetFlow?

GadgetFlow is a live stock board for gadget resellers. Every supplier gets a
**private link**. They post their phones, laptops and accessories from that
link. Everything they post shows up immediately on the **public catalog**.
Buyers — usually on WhatsApp — open the catalog or a single product card and
instantly know what is **In Stock** or **Sold**.

Three kinds of people use it:

| Role         | What they do                                              | How they get in                       |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Admin**    | Creates suppliers, sees everything, manages all stock     | Logs in with the master admin link    |
| **Supplier** | Posts and updates only their own products                 | Opens their personal supplier link    |
| **Buyer**    | Browses the catalog, taps WhatsApp to ask about an item   | Just opens the homepage — no login    |

There is **no password and no sign-up**. Access is controlled by the URL.

---

## The three URLs you need to know

Assume your site is hosted at `https://your-site.com`. Then:

1. **Public catalog** — `https://your-site.com/`
   Anyone can open this. It shows every product in real time. Read-only.

2. **Admin dashboard** — `https://your-site.com/supplier/ADMIN-SECRET-2025`
   This is the master link. It shows every supplier's stock in one place and
   has a **+ New Supplier** button. Treat this link like a password — anyone
   with it can add or remove products.

3. **Supplier dashboard** — `https://your-site.com/supplier/<CODE>`
   Each supplier has a unique 8-character code generated by the admin.
   They only see and edit their own products.

If someone opens a supplier link with a wrong or expired code, they get a
clear **"This link is not valid. Contact your admin."** screen — they cannot
get into the system.

## For the admin

### 1. Logging in
Open the admin link:
`https://your-site.com/supplier/ADMIN-SECRET-2025`

You will land on the **Admin Dashboard**. The page header says
"◈ ADMIN DASHBOARD" so you always know where you are.

### 2. Creating a supplier
1. Tap **+ NEW SUPPLIER** in the top-right.
2. Type the supplier's name (e.g. "Tunde Gadgets").
3. Tap **GENERATE LINK**.
4. The modal now shows the full private link, for example:
   `https://your-site.com/supplier/A8K3MQX9`
5. Tap **COPY LINK** and send it to that supplier on WhatsApp.

They keep that link forever. It is their door into the system.

### 3. Managing stock
On the admin dashboard you see **every product across every supplier**. Each
card shows a small **supplier name badge** in the top-left so you know who
posted what.

You can:
- Tap **✓ Mark Sold** on any card to flip it to Sold.
- Tap **↺ Mark Available** to bring a sold item back to In Stock.
- Tap **+ ADD PRODUCT** to add an item yourself (it will be attached to the
  "Admin" supplier).

### 4. Filtering
At the top of the grid: **ALL · AVAILABLE · SOLD**. Tap any tab to filter.

---

## For suppliers

### 1. Open your link
The admin will send you a link that looks like:
`https://your-site.com/supplier/A8K3MQX9`

Save it. Bookmark it. Pin the WhatsApp chat where you got it.
**This link is your login** — don't share it.

### 2. Posting a product
1. Tap **+ ADD PRODUCT** in the top-right.
2. Tap the dashed image box to upload a photo (or drag and drop on desktop).
3. Fill in:
   - **PRODUCT NAME** — e.g. *iPhone 14 Pro*
   - **SPECS** — e.g. *128GB · Space Black · Sealed*
   - **PRICE (NGN)** — just the number, e.g. *780000*
4. Tap **PUBLISH PRODUCT**.

The product is live the instant you tap publish — both on the public catalog
and on the admin's screen.

### 3. Marking sold
When a product sells, tap **✓ Mark Sold** under that card. The card fades to
a soft "no longer available" state. If you made a mistake, tap
**↺ Mark Available** and it comes right back.

### 4. What you see vs. what others see
- You see **only your own** products on your dashboard.
- The admin sees **all** products including yours.
- Buyers on the homepage see everyone's products, but they cannot edit
  anything.

## For buyers

### 1. Open the catalog
Just go to `https://your-site.com/`. No login.

### 2. Browse
Products are arranged in an editorial grid — tall portrait photos, product
name, specs, and price in **₦ Naira**. The top-right corner of every card
tells you the status:

- 🟢 **● AVAILABLE** — still in stock
- 🔴 **● SOLD** — already gone

Use the **ALL · AVAILABLE · SOLD** tabs to filter.

### 3. Ask about a product
Tap the **WhatsApp icon** on any card. WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled
message already showing:

```
*iPhone 14 Pro*
128GB · Space Black · Sealed
Price: ₦780,000
Status: ✅ In Stock

View full catalog: https://your-site.com
```

Pick the contact you want to send it to. That's it.

## Real-time, explained simply

GadgetFlow uses Supabase real-time. In plain English:

- When a supplier publishes a new product, it appears on the buyer's screen
  **without refresh**.
- When a supplier marks something sold, every open page updates **within a
  second** — the public catalog, the admin dashboard, the supplier's own view.
- You never need to press refresh. Just leave the tab open.

## What to do if…

**A supplier loses their link.** Open the admin dashboard, generate a new
supplier (same name is fine), and send them the new link. (The old one still
works if you remember the code; for security, treat lost codes as compromised
and stop using them.)

**You posted a wrong product.** Mark it sold to hide it from buyer attention,
or ask the admin to delete it directly in Supabase (the public app currently
only flips status — full delete is via the database).

**A buyer says a "sold" item is back.** Tap **↺ Mark Available** on the card.

**The admin link gets shared by accident.** Change the constant
`ADMIN_CODE = 'ADMIN-SECRET-2025'` in `app/supplier/[code]/page.jsx`,
redeploy, and your old admin URL stops working.

**An image fails to upload.** Check that the Supabase Storage bucket
`product-images` exists and is public. The included `supabase/schema.sql`
sets this up for you.

## Setting up your own copy (one-time, technical)

You only do this once.

1. **Create a Supabase project** at supabase.com.
2. In the Supabase SQL editor, paste the contents of
   `supabase/schema.sql` and run it. This creates the tables, the storage
   bucket, the policies, **and the admin supplier**.
3. **Copy** your project's `URL` and `anon` key from
   *Project Settings → API*.
4. **Deploy on Vercel.** Import the repo and add two environment variables:
   - `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`
   - `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`
5. **Open the admin link**: `https://your-site.com/supplier/ADMIN-SECRET-2025`
6. Generate supplier links and start posting stock.

| -------------------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------- |

That's the whole product. If something feels broken, it almost always boils
down to one of three things: the wrong link, the env vars on Vercel, or the
SQL script wasn't run. Fix those in order and you're back in business.
